System Overview

The FRIC Portal (Faculty Research Incentive Cell) is an enterprise academic governance and incentive management platform engineered for P. P. Savani University (PPSU). It digitizes the end-to-end lifecycle of faculty research incentive claims across publications (Q1–Q4, Scopus, SCI, ABDC), granted/published patents, books, citations, and research grants. The platform enforces multi-stage sequential verification, role isolation, automatic policy calculations, and official signed Provost PDF sanction order generation.

Multi-Stage Verification Pipeline
Stage Assigned Authority / Role Primary Verification Scope & Responsibilities Stage Key
1 Initial Faculty Member (Claimant) Submits claim metadata, uploads supporting proofs (PDF), accepts statutory declaration, and receives live incentive preview. SUBMITTED
2 Stage 1 Head of Department (HOD) Validates institutional affiliation, departmental workload compliance, and endorses initial research output proofs. HOD_PRINCIPAL
3 Stage 2 Research Committee Member 1 Audits indexing database authenticity (Scopus, SCI, Web of Science, ABDC, UGC CARE), journal quartile, impact factor, and DOI. COMMITTEE_M1
4 Stage 3 Research Committee Member 2 Verifies author sequence (First, Corresponding, Co-author) and calculates internal PPSU revenue distribution split. COMMITTEE_M2
5 Stage 4 Research Committee Convenor / Governor Audits annual university research budget caps, enforces policy claim limits per faculty, and submits final payout recommendation. GOVERNOR
6 Stage 5 Provost (Executive Authority) Grants final financial payout approval, executes institutional sanction, and generates official verifiable PDF Sanction Order. APPROVED
Return & Resubmit Mechanism
  • Mandatory Return Remarks: Verifiers cannot return a claim without entering detailed written feedback specifying missing proofs or required corrections.
  • Faculty Edit & Resubmit: The claimant receives an automated notification, updates claim data, re-uploads supporting documents, and resubmits.
  • Direct Stage Resume: Resubmitted claims route directly back to the exact verifier who returned them, preserving all prior stage approvals.
  • Self-Review Bypass: If a verifier or HOD submits a personal claim, their own stage is automatically bypassed to prevent conflict of interest.
